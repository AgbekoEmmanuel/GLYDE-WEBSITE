### FAQ SECTION

**Section Tag:** `/ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS`

**Heading:** "Got questions?"

**FAQ Items (8):**

**Q1:** How do I pay for my ticket?
**A:** You can pay using Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, AirtelTigo Money, Telecel Cash) or by card through the GLYDE app. All payments are processed securely.

**Q2:** Can I cancel or change my booking?
**A:** Yes. You can cancel or change your booking up to 30 minutes before your departure time. Cancellations within 30 minutes of departure are non-refundable.

**Q3:** What happens if the bus is late?
**A:** GLYDE operates on a fixed schedule. In the rare case of a delay, you'll be notified via the app immediately with an updated arrival time.

**Q4:** How many seats are available per bus?
**A:** Each GLYDE bus has a fixed passenger capacity. Once a departure is fully booked, it shows as unavailable. We recommend booking in advance during peak hours.

**Q5:** Are the buses air-conditioned?
**A:** Yes. All GLYDE buses are air-conditioned for passenger comfort.

**Q6:** Can I book for someone else?
**A:** Yes. You can book a seat for another person. Their name and phone number will be required at booking.

**Q7:** What if I miss my bus?
**A:** If you miss your scheduled departure, you can rebook on the next available departure at the standard fare. Missed rides are non-refundable.

**Q8:** When will the app be available?
**A:** The GLYDE app is currently in final development and will be available on iOS and Android soon. Join our waitlist to be notified first.
