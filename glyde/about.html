/ Our Story

We've been stuck in the same traffic.

GLYDE was born from a simple frustration — the daily chaos of commuting in Accra. Overloaded trotros, unpredictable waits, and zero accountability. We knew there had to be a better way.

We started by asking commuters what they actually needed: reliability, safety, and a price they could afford. Not a luxury product. Not a premium service. Just a good, dependable bus that shows up when it says it will.

So we built it. A tech-enabled bus network with scheduled departures, app-based booking, vetted drivers, and a commitment to safety at every level. Starting with Accra, growing with Ghana.
