Rigorous Driver Selection

Every GLYDE driver undergoes background checks, driving record review, and a minimum experience requirement before they ever touch the wheel.

Roadworthy by Requirement

All buses hold valid DVLA roadworthiness certificates, commercial insurance, and undergo scheduled maintenance checks — no exceptions.

Fully Insured Journeys

Every GLYDE bus carries commercial passenger insurance. Every rider is covered for the full duration of their journey.

Emergency Ready

Every bus carries a first aid kit, fire extinguisher, and warning triangles. Every driver is trained on incident response and passenger evacuation.

Zero Overcrowding

GLYDE enforces strict passenger capacity limits. No standing passengers. No overloading. Your app booking guarantees your seat.

Transparent Reporting

All incidents — no matter how minor — are logged, investigated, and reported. We take accountability seriously.

/ DRIVER REQUIREMENTS

Not just any driver.
A GLYDE driver.

We set the bar high because your safety depends on the person behind the wheel.

  • Valid Ghanaian driver's licence (appropriate class for commercial passenger bus)
  • Minimum age: 25 years
  • Minimum 3 years experience on similar vehicle class
  • Background and criminal record check
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • GLYDE defensive driving training (completed before first shift)
  • Customer service training
  • Emergency and evacuation procedure training
  • Zero-tolerance alcohol and drug policy
  • Regular performance reviews

/ BUS STANDARDS

Every bus earns its road.

  • DVLA registered as commercial passenger vehicle
  • Valid roadworthiness certificate
  • Commercial passenger insurance (correct capacity coverage)
  • Yellow commercial plates
  • Fire extinguisher (serviced and accessible)
  • First aid kit (stocked)
  • Warning triangles
  • Scheduled maintenance log (inspected every X km)
  • Maximum capacity enforced — no overloading
  • Air conditioning operational
